## 1. Concrete Protocol: How to Add the Next Case Study

When adding a new machine learning or data engineering project to this portfolio, follow this 4-step workflow:

1. **Maintain Context in Claude Project:**  
   Open the configured **Claude Portfolio Tutor Project** (which already contains the Voice Card: *"Direct, technical, plain-spoken, evidence-backed, zero buzzwords"* and technical stack details).
2. **Conduct the 3-Beat Interview:**  
   Use Claude to interview yourself about the new project by prompting:
   > *"Interview me about my new project one question at a time to extract: 1. The Problem, 2. What I Did & Decided, 3. What Came of It (empirical results)."*
3. **Draft using the 3-Beat Structure:**  
   Format the resulting case study strictly into the established shape:
   * **Beat 1: The Problem** (Plain-spoken business/engineering pain point).
   * **Beat 2: What I Did & Decided** (Specific stack, DuckDB/ML choices, and tradeoffs).
   * **Beat 3: What Came of It** (Concrete quantitative output, $R^2$ scores, or latency reductions).
4. **Deploy & Update Sitemap:**  
   Append the new case study to `work/case_studies_framing.md` and link it under the core `/work` section in `portfolio_sitemap.md`.
