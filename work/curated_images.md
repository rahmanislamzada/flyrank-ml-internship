## 2. Real Capture vs. AI Decision Log

* **Decision 1 (Data Processing Output):** Used a real cropped Colab screenshot of the DuckDB aggregated DataFrame instead of an AI-generated mock dashboard image.  
  * *Reason:* Hiring managers instantly recognize fake AI dashboard mockups. Showing real Python/Pandas dataframe outputs proves the code actually executed and returned data.
* **Decision 2 (Profile Headshot):** Used an unedited real photograph instead of a polished AI avatar.  
  * *Reason:* Establishes genuine identity and personal transparency.
