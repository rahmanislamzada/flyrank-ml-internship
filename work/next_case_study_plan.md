## 1. How to Add the Next Case Study (The 3-Beat Workflow)
To maintain momentum without rebuilding the portfolio site from scratch, future portfolio additions will strictly follow this standardized 3-beat structure:

1. **Beat 1: The Problem (Context & Friction)**
   * *What to document:* The core technical bottleneck, business metric gap, or data challenge.
2. **Beat 2: What Was Built (Action & Architecture)**
   * *What to document:* The ML pipeline, model design, data transformation (e.g., DuckDB/Python), and human-in-the-loop decision boundaries.
3. **Beat 3: What Came of It (Measured Impact)**
   * *What to document:* Concrete evaluation metrics (F1-score, precision, processing efficiency lift) compared against baseline heuristics.
