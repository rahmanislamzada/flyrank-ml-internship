# Phase: Foundations — Visual Identity & Framing Audit (Week 3)

---

## 1. Visual Identity Choices (Consistency, Not Talent)
* **Typography:** System font stack (`-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto`) ensuring fast load times, zero font flicker, and maximum readability across devices.
* **Color Hierarchy:** 
  * Background: Clean neutral light gray (`#f9fafb`)
  * Surface/Cards: Pure white (`#ffffff`) with subtle borders to structure content
  * Text: High-contrast charcoal (`#111827`, `#1f2937`)
  * Accent/Action: Deep technical blue (`#2563eb`) reserved strictly for interactive links and submit actions.
* **Layout Discipline:** Fixed-width container (max 900px), consistent vertical rhythm (2rem spacing), and clean visual scaffolding.

---

## 2. Framing Rule: "Frame the Work, Never Upstage It"
* **Content First:** Avoided distracting animations, complex parallax scrolling, or heavy decorative graphics that detract from technical proof.
* **Data Clarity:** Key ML metrics (e.g., F1-score lift, precision, data scale) and architecture diagrams are framed inside distinct, scannable white cards.
* **Real Proof vs. AI Fluff:** Prioritized actual terminal execution commands, mathematical evaluation tables, and concrete schema pipelines over generic AI-generated stock illustrations.

---

## 3. Visual & Asset Selection Judgment
* **Image/Graphic Strategy:** Real system flowcharts and code blocks take precedence over generated imagery.
* **Rejection Criteria:** Rejected overloaded futuristic 3D hero banners in favor of minimal, clean structural scaffolding that signals engineering rigor to technical reviewers.
